LOVE CITY · MISSION FOREVER
An Invite Wonderland original playable invitation.

The original boulevard, island, venue, signage, traffic, coupe, aircraft, yacht, dolphins, costumes and articulated people are built from geometry in src/art. The surface and frond artwork is authored for this project. The sky and car-reflection atlas are color-graded derivatives of Poly Haven's Kloppenheim 06 (Pure Sky), licensed CC0.
Source https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_06_puresky
License https://polyhaven.com/license
The sky, reflection and frond preparation scripts are in src/art/coast.

The six photographic story illustrations (boulevard, groom with envelope, bride at the airfield, wedding terrace, yacht and glasshouse) were generated for this project using OpenAI ImageGen. They depict fictional people and a fictional city. The guest and creator presentations use an original synthesized score, vehicle sounds, surf and musical cues. Optional speech is generated by the visitor’s browser or supplied as client audio. No Rockstar images, music, maps, models or recordings ship with the experience.

The live bride and groom use project-specific head meshes and photographic materials created with OpenAI ImageGen, with attire and styling guided by the supplied Sunset Yacht Wedding Portrait. Facial landmarks were prepared offline with Google's MediaPipe Face Landmarker. The live application loads the baked assets from this package.
Source https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_landmarker/python

The walking torso and arm curves were retargeted from take 07_01 of the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database.
Source https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/subjects/07/
